# Sources, attribution, and verification

Research date: 2026-10-27. Primary reference: **Laws of UX**, created by **Jon Yablonski**, https://lawsofux.com/. This independent package has no claimed affiliation or endorsement.

## Structure and coverage

The English homepage contains 30 linked concept pages. Each provides a concept statement and takeaways, with origins, examples, or further reading where available. The site also has Articles, Book, Cards, and Info sections, language variants, and downloadable/store assets. The package covers the complete 30-concept English homepage inventory and the eight public articles linked from Articles on the research date. It does not reproduce or claim access to the paid book, 54-card deck, poster assets, or every external bibliography source.

All 30 detail-page GET requests returned HTTP 200. Definitions, takeaways, and available examples/origins were consulted. Original source URLs appear in every entry and the [inventory](ux-laws-index.md). The inventory was established before writing the entries. [The manifest](../tests/source-manifest.json) stores request status and HTML hashes for traceability; hashes are snapshots, not correctness proofs. Original page text is not bundled.

## Interpretation and limitations

Definitions are original concise explanations of concepts. UI recommendations, safeguards, code, workflows, categories, priorities, and examples are this package’s synthesis, not quotations or claims of experimental findings. Preserve the difference between historical models, empirical tendencies, and heuristics. No claim is made that every origin citation was independently peer-reviewed or every linked external study was opened. Research checked the English content; translation accuracy was not assessed. No primary concept page or linked public article failed access. Live sources can change; the verification date limits completeness claims.
